Shadow Levels

In dark mode, shadows are subtle -- elevation is communicated through luminance stacking rather than cast shadows. Multi-layer stacks for popovers and dialogs.

Level 0 - FlatNo shadow. Deepest canvas.
Level 1 - SubtleToolbar buttons, micro-elevation.
Level 2b - InsetRecessed panels, inner shadow.
Level 3 - RingBorder-as-shadow for cards.
Level 4 - ElevatedFloating elements, dropdowns.
Level 5 - DialogMulti-layer stack for modals.
Level 6 - FocusKeyboard focus ring shadow.
