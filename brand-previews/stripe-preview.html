Shadow Levels

The signature blue-tinted dual-layer shadow system. Far shadow creates atmospheric depth, near shadow anchors the element.

Level 0 - FlatNo shadow. Page background.
Level 1 - AmbientSubtle card lift, hover hints.
Level 2 - StandardCards, content panels.
Level 3 - ElevatedBlue-tinted far + near parallax.
Level 4 - DeepDark blue dual-layer for modals.
